Privacy Policy

Blockquarium · Effective 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Blockquarium game ("the game", "we") uses, and why. The game is a puzzle game for Android. It has no accounts and no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device: the levels you have unlocked, your coins, and your settings (music, sounds and vibration). This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game, or choosing Reset progress in Settings, deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward (for example, coins or another try at a level). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device. This can include your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and settings, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings under Google › Ads.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Your progress stays on your device. Information collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's security practices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: blockquarium@proton.me
